# Third-party marks

The app shows two marks that are not Soredemo's, only to say which agent the person has chosen to
drive from the Agent tab. They are used to identify the product, not to suggest that its maker
endorses, sponsors or works with Soredemo.

| Asset | Mark | Owner | Taken from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Soredemo/Resources/Assets.xcassets/ClaudeMark.imageset/claude.svg` | the Claude symbol | Anthropic | svgl.app, `claude-ai-icon.svg`, 2026-11-08 |
| `Soredemo/Resources/Assets.xcassets/OpenAIMark.imageset/openai.svg` | the OpenAI symbol (for Codex) | OpenAI | svgl.app, `openai.svg`, 2026-11-08 |

Both were checked on arrival: plain SVG, no script, no external reference. Neither is altered. If
either owner's brand guidelines ask for a change, or for the mark to be removed, the change is made
here and in the asset, and this table says when.
